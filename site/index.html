Why you'll love it

Seven ridiculous bosses

Disco Man, the Caffeinated Sloth, Dr Mantis and more, each with several forms and illustrated comic scenes.

Seven living arenas

From a burning firehouse square to a neon dance floor that bursts open as zombies rise through the tiles.

Instant to learn

Slide to move, hold FIRE, tap the grenade. Let ten zombies through and the camp falls.

Your arcade, your way

A glowing LED handheld, 24 console skins, joystick colours and unlockable character skins.

Special weapons

Light the floor targets to win a boss-themed weapon: a laser pulse, a water pulse, a bass cannon and more.

Free. No ads. No login.

Pick a name and play. A global leaderboard is waiting, and the game also works offline.
