# Zombie Smash: closed test kit (12+ Android testers for 14 days)

Google needs **at least 12 people opted in to your closed test for at least 14 days** before you can apply for production. Invite **15 to 20**, because some people never get round to it.

## 1. Before you invite anyone (you, in Play Console)
The dashboard says "To start a closed test, finish setting up your game", so first complete the declarations (privacy policy, app access, ads, content rating, target audience, Data safety) and save the store listing. Then:
1. **Test and release > Testing > Closed testing > Create track** (call it "Friends").
2. **Testers** tab > create an email list "Zombie Smash testers" and add the testers' Google emails (comma or new line).
3. **Releases**: promote your existing internal release to this track (Promote release), add the release notes, and roll out.
4. Copy the **opt-in link** from the Testers tab. Send it to everyone.

## 2. Who to ask (real people only)
- Friends and family with Android phones, including the friends whose gamer tags inspired the bosses.
- Colleagues, customers and their families.
- Social posts to your own followers (Facebook, Instagram stories, WhatsApp status).
- Tester-swap communities, where developers test each other's apps: Reddit r/AndroidClosedTesting, r/TestersCommunity, and Discord servers for indie developers. Real people testing for real is fine; paid "tester services" are not.
- Ask each person for **the Google account email they use on their Android phone** (usually Gmail). It must be that account.

## 3. Message to send (copy and edit)
**Short text / WhatsApp**
> Hi! I've made a retro zombie arcade game called Zombie Smash and I need 12 Android testers for 2 weeks to get it on Google Play. It takes 2 minutes: reply with the Gmail you use on your Android phone, then I'll send a link. Tap "Become a tester", install it, and keep it installed for 14 days. Playing a few times would be amazing. Thank you!

**Email**
> Subject: Can you help test my game for 2 weeks? (Android)
>
> Hi [name],

> 2. I'll add you and send a link. Open it on the device, tap "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play".

> It's free, with no ads and no login. Thank you so much!

**Social post**
> Android people: I need 12 testers for my new retro zombie shooter, Zombie Smash. 2 minutes to join, then just keep it installed for 14 days. Comment or message me your Gmail and I'll send the link. Free, no ads.

## 4. Instructions for testers (send after they reply)
1. Use an **Android** phone or tablet signed in to the Gmail address you gave me.
2. Open this link on that device: [paste the closed-test opt-in link].
3. Tap **Become a tester**, then **Download it on Google Play** and install it.
4. **Keep it installed for 14 days.** Please open it a few times. Don't opt out.
5. Anything wrong? Email support@zc2.quest and say what phone you have.

## 6. Timeline
- **Day 0:** link goes out. Add everyone's email to the list before sending the link.
- **Day 2:** remind anyone who hasn't opted in.
- **Day 3 to 5:** check Play Console. Aim for **12 or more opted in**. Recruit more if you're under.
- **Day 7:** check the count again; thank people.
- **Day 14 (count from when you first have 12 opted in):** Dashboard > **Apply for production access**.
- Then Google reviews your application and the app, which can take several days.

## 7. Production access questions (prepare answers)
Google asks about your test: how you recruited testers (friends, family, community), what feedback you got, and what you fixed. Keep a short note of every bug or comment as it arrives.
